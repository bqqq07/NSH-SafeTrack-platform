Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026
SafeTrack is a workforce and HSE (Health, Safety & Environment) management platform for construction and industrial companies. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use our app and services.

1. Information We Collect

When you use SafeTrack, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information — name, employee ID or code, and role within your organization, used for authentication and access control.
  • Photos and Media — uploaded voluntarily by users for HSE observations, toolbox talk sign-in sheets, JSO closures, near-miss reports, and inspection records.
  • Activity Data — safety observations, daily check-ins, TBT attendance, BBS card counts, near-miss reports, and other records submitted through the app.
  • Device Information — basic technical data (device type, OS version) used to ensure app compatibility. We do not collect precise location data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app's core workforce and HSE management features.
  • To authenticate users and grant access to authorized features based on role.
  • To generate HSE performance reports and KPI dashboards for supervisors.
  • To help organizations track and improve site safety activities.
  • To improve the reliability and performance of the platform.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the app's core functionality.

3. Data Isolation Between Organizations

Each organization (company) that uses SafeTrack has its data fully isolated from all other organizations on the platform. Users from one organization cannot access or view data belonging to another organization under any circumstances.

4. Data Sharing

Your data is only accessible to:

  • Authorized users within your own organization (based on their assigned role).
  • The platform's hosting infrastructure required to operate the service.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party.

5. Photos and Media

The app requests access to your device's camera and photo library solely to allow you to upload evidence photos for safety records. Photos are stored on secured servers and are accessible only to authorized users within your organization.

6. Data Retention

Your personal data and submitted records are retained for as long as your account and organization remain active on the platform. If an account is deactivated, data may be retained for up to 24 months for record-keeping purposes, then securely deleted or anonymized upon request.

7. Data Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored on secured servers. Access to the system requires valid credentials and role-based authorization. We implement commercially reasonable security measures to protect your information.

8. Children's Privacy

SafeTrack is intended for use by employed adults in professional work environments. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 16.

9. Your Rights

You may request to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time by contacting us at the email address below. Your organization's administrator can also manage user data directly within the platform. We will respond within a reasonable time frame.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
