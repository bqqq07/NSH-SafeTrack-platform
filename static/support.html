Getting Started

SafeTrack is available to any construction or industrial company. Register your company for free and start managing your workforce and safety activities in minutes.

Features

  • Employee management and badge tracking
  • Attendance monitoring and reporting
  • Leave and HR request management
  • Daily and weekly supervisor evaluations
  • HSE daily check-in and location tracking
  • Safety observations — unsafe acts, unsafe conditions, positive observations
  • JSO closure records with photo evidence
  • Toolbox Talk (TBT) with attendance list and PDF export
  • Near Miss reporting
  • BBS (Behaviour-Based Safety) daily card counts
  • HSE inspections and Permit to Work (PTW) tracking
  • Weekly and monthly HSE reports with PDF export
  • KPI dashboard with trend indicators for supervisors
